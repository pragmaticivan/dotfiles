# Provenance of the reference files

`writing-rules.md`, `word-selection.md`, `dictionary-approved.md`, and
`dictionary-unapproved.md` are condensed from **ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical
English, Issue 9 (2025-01-15)**, published by the AeroSpace and Defence
Industries Association of Europe (ASD). The standard is available at
<https://asd-ste100.org/>.

They were adapted from the `asd-ste100` skill in
<https://github.com/cfcosta/writing-styles> (MIT licence, © Cainã Costa), which
is the upstream source of this skill's structure.

`software-domain.md` and `SKILL.md` are original to this repository.

The condensed files exist to let a model apply the rules and look words up.
They are not a substitute for the specification: for certification work, an
audit, or any question of exact wording, read the published standard. Where a
condensed entry and the standard disagree, the standard wins.
