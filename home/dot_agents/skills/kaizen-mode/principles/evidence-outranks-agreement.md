# Evidence outranks agreement

*Apply when you assess agent findings, resolve disagreements, or decide whether a review is complete.*

Treat each finding as a claim to check. Agreement does not establish truth. Different models can share the same error.

- Collect independent findings before reviewers see other conclusions or the author's defense.
- Give reviewers the requirements and access to relevant source. Do not confuse independence with missing context.
- Resolve factual disagreements with a check that distinguishes the competing claims.
- Keep impact severity, evidence strength, and reviewer agreement separate.
- Accept a supported finding even when only one reviewer reports it.
- Record uncertainty when the available evidence cannot settle a claim. Do not convert uncertainty into approval or dismissal.

Use the [adversarial review protocol](../references/adversarial-review.md) for the finding record, verification steps, and stop rules.
